🌿 Grounding Exercise

Use the 5-4-3-2-1 technique to calm anxiety and stay present

How it works

This grounding technique helps you focus on the present moment by engaging your five senses. Notice and describe each item thoughtfully. Take your time.

👁️

5 Things You Can See

Look around and name 5 things you can see right now

✋

4 Things You Can Touch

Notice the texture and temperature of 4 things around you

👂

3 Things You Can Hear

Listen carefully and identify 3 sounds

👃

2 Things You Can Smell

What scents can you notice? (Or think of 2 favorite scents)

👅

1 Thing You Can Taste

Notice any taste in your mouth, or think of a favorite flavor

✨ Exercise Complete!

Great job staying present! You've successfully completed the 5-4-3-2-1 grounding exercise. How do you feel now?
