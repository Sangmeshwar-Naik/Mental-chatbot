Immediate help and resources for difficult moments

⚠️ If you're in immediate danger

Please call emergency services (911 in US, 112 in EU, 999 in UK) or go to your nearest emergency room. Your life is valuable, and help is available 24/7.

🧘 Immediate Grounding Techniques

These exercises can help you feel more present and calm right now.

5-4-3-2-1 Grounding
Notice 5 things you can see, 4 things you can touch, 3 things you can hear, 2 things you can smell, and 1 thing you can taste.
Box Breathing (4-4-4-4)
Breathe in for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, exhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds. Repeat 4 times.
Cold Water Technique
Splash cold water on your face or hold an ice cube. The shock can help reset your nervous system and bring you to the present moment.
Progressive Muscle Relaxation
Tense each muscle group for 5 seconds, then release. Start with your toes and work up to your head.

📝 Create Your Safety Plan

Steps to Take When in Crisis:

  1. Recognize your warning signs (thoughts, images, mood, behavior)
  2. Use coping strategies (exercise, meditation, journaling)
  3. Reach out to supportive people
  4. Contact mental health professionals
  5. Call crisis hotlines if needed
  6. Make your environment safe (remove potential means of self-harm)
