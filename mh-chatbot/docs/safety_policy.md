# Safety Policy - AI Mental Health Chatbot

## Purpose

This safety policy outlines protocols for crisis detection, intervention, and user protection within the AI Mental Health Chatbot platform.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

**This platform is NOT a substitute for professional mental health care.**
- Not for emergency situations
- Not for diagnosis or treatment
- Should be used under clinical supervision
- Designed as a supplementary support tool

## Crisis Detection Protocol

### 1. Automated Keyword Detection

The platform monitors conversations for crisis indicators:

**Critical Keywords** (Immediate intervention):
- suicide, suicidal
- kill myself
- end my life
- want to die
- better off dead

**High-Risk Keywords** (Urgent attention):
- self-harm
- cut myself
- hurt myself
- overdose
- jump off

**Moderate-Risk Keywords** (Monitoring):
- hopeless
- worthless
- burden
- give up
- can't go on

### 2. Risk Level Assessment

Messages are automatically classified:

- **CRITICAL**: Immediate suicidal ideation or intent
- **HIGH**: Active thoughts of self-harm
- **MODERATE**: Significant distress, hopelessness
- **LOW**: General mental health challenges

### 3. Automated Response Protocol

**When crisis keywords are detected:**

1. **User** receives immediate crisis resources:
   - US: 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
   - International: +1-800-273-8255
   - Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741

2. **Conversation** is flagged for admin review

3. **Admin notification** sent via:
   - Email to designated crisis response team
   - SMS to on-call clinician (if configured)
   - Dashboard alert

4. **User's risk level** updated in database

### 4. Manual Intervention

**Admin Actions:**
- Review flagged conversation immediately
- Add clinician notes
- Assess if direct outreach is needed
- Contact emergency services if danger is imminent
- Follow up with user after crisis resolution

## User Protection Measures

### Privacy & Confidentiality
- All conversations encrypted
- Anonymized data for crisis review
- HIPAA/GDPR compliant data handling
- Clear privacy policy accessible to users

### Content Moderation
- AI responses reviewed for harmful content
- Empathetic, non-judgmental language
- Avoid giving direct medical advice
- Encourage professional help seeking

### User Consent
- Explicit consent during registration
- Crisis protocol explained upfront
- Option to opt-out of crisis monitoring (with warnings)
- Transparency about admin access to conversations

## Prohibited Content

The platform prohibits:
- Medical diagnosis
- Prescription of medications
- Replacement of licensed therapy
- Encouragement of harmful behaviors
- Sharing of user data without consent

## Crisis Response Team

### Roles & Responsibilities

**Admin/Clinician:**
- Monitor flagged conversations
- Respond to crisis alerts within 15 minutes (business hours)
- Document interventions
- Coordinate with emergency services if needed

**On-Call Protocol:**
- 24/7 on-call clinician (recommended)
- Escalation path for after-hours
- Emergency contact list maintained

## Reporting & Documentation

### All Crisis Events Must Include:
- User ID and timestamp
- Risk level assessment
- Conversation excerpt (context)
- Actions taken
- Outcome/follow-up

### Dashboard Metrics:
- Number of crisis detections (daily/weekly)
- Response times
- False positive rate
- User outcomes (if available)

## Continuous Improvement

### Regular Reviews:
- Quarterly audit of crisis keywords
- False positive analysis
- User feedback on interventions
- AI prompt refinement for safety

### Training:
- Staff training on crisis intervention
- Regular updates to safety protocols
- Practice drills for emergency scenarios

## Legal & Ethical Considerations

### Mandatory Reporting

Comply with local laws regarding:
- Imminent danger to self or others
- Child abuse disclosures
- Elder abuse disclosures

### Liability Limitations

Clear disclaimers:
- Platform limitations
- AI technology constraints
- User responsibility for seeking professional help
- No guarantee of crisis prevention
